1. Login

Make sure you are signed into your verified ReuseHub account. If you are new, registration takes under a minute.

2. Click "Give an Item"

Click the green "Give an Item" button located in the top navigation bar from any page.

3. Upload Photos

Provide clear, authentic photos showing the item's current state, including any minor blemishes or wear.

4. Enter Details & Condition

Give your listing a clear title and description. Select the condition (New, Like New, Good, Fair, or Needs Repair).

5. Choose Category

Categorize your item (e.g. Furniture, Electronics, Books, Clothing) to help neighbors find it quickly.

6. Set Pickup Location

Search using our OpenStreetMap Nominatim search for a neighborhood landmark or street to keep your exact home private.

7. Submit Listing

Hit "Publish Listing". Your item is immediately visible to local community members looking for goods.

8. Manage Requests

Review incoming requests from your dashboard or item page, accept the best match, and coordinate handoff.

Requests Workflow

Managing Requests

Clear, transparent communication ensures smooth handoffs for both donors and requesters.

For Item Owners
  • View Requests: Check your item detail page or My Listings to see all neighbors who expressed interest.
  • Accept Request: When you find a suitable recipient, click "Accept Request" to share contact details and reserve the item.
  • Reject / Pass: If someone cannot pick up promptly, you can decline to allow other requesters a turn.
  • Contact Requester: Use the provided contact details or email to agree on a pickup time and place.
  • Mark Item as Given Away: Once collected, click "Mark Given Away" to celebrate another diverted item and close new requests.
For Requesters
  • View Requests: Visit your My Requests dashboard to monitor the status of every item you've requested.
  • Check Status: See if your request is Pending, Accepted, or Given to Another Member.
  • Cancel Request: If your plans change and you no longer need the item, promptly cancel so others can receive it.
  • Confirm Collection: Show up on time for agreed pickup and thank your neighbor for their generosity!
Location & Privacy

Pickup & Location Help

ReuseHub uses OpenStreetMap Nominatim to provide fast, privacy-preserving address suggestions.

How to Set Your Pickup Location

  1. Search for your neighborhood or landmark: Type a few letters of your town, neighborhood, or public landmark (e.g. "Koramangala 4th Block" or "Central Park West").
  2. Select from Nominatim suggestions: Choose the best matching location from the live dropdown list. The system automatically secures geographic coordinates.
  3. Use "Current Location": You can also tap the "Use My Current Location" button to automatically reverse-geocode your approximate area.
  4. Privacy Protection: ReuseHub intentionally does not require your exact apartment or door number on public listings. Only general neighborhood areas are shown publicly.
  5. Coordinating Collection: Once you accept a request, you can privately share your exact door number or arrange a meetup at a nearby public spot.
📍

Pickup Safety Tip

For bulky items (furniture, large appliances), arranging porch or lobby pickup in daylight is recommended. For portable items, busy public landmarks like a coffee shop or library plaza are ideal.

Account & Settings

Account Help

Manage your account credentials, profile information, and preferences.

How do I register for an account?
Click "Join" in the top-right header navigation. Enter your chosen username, email address, and a secure password, then submit. You will be logged in immediately and ready to give or request items!
How do I log in to my account?
Click "Sign in" in the top header. Enter your registered username and password. If you navigated to a restricted page (like listing an item), you will be redirected automatically once signed in.
How do I change my password?
You can update your security credentials anytime from your Profile page or via the password reset option on the login page.
How do I edit my profile?
Click on your username badge in the top navigation and select Profile. Here you can upload a profile avatar, update your display bio, and set your default neighborhood location.
How do I delete or deactivate my account?
If you wish to remove your account or delete your listings, please submit a brief request using the Contact Support Form below with the subject "Account Deactivation". Our moderation team will process your request within 24 hours.
Frequently Asked Questions

General Platform FAQ

Quick answers to the most frequently asked questions across the platform.

What is ReuseHub?
ReuseHub is a non-profit, community-driven platform designed to connect people who have surplus, usable items with neighbors who can use or restore them. Our goal is to promote a circular economy, reduce waste, and build closer local communities.
What items can I give away?
You can give away books, electronics, clothing, furniture, home decor, sports gear, kitchenware, baby supplies, musical instruments, tools, and repairable gadgets. All items must be legal, clean, and safe. Hazardous goods, weapons, and expired medications are prohibited.
How do I request an item?
When browsing any item detail page, click the green "Request Item" button. Write a friendly note explaining why you need the item and your availability for pickup. The donor will review requests and notify you if selected.
How does the "Repairable" badge work?
The Repairable badge indicates that an item has minor flaws (e.g. a broken plug, loose joint, or cosmetic scratch) but can easily be restored or repurposed. Tinkerers and makers can filter by "Repairable" to rescue items that would otherwise end up in scrap.
How do I contact the item owner?
To protect member privacy from unsolicited spam, contact information is exchanged only after a donor formally accepts an item request. Once accepted, both parties receive communication instructions.
How do I cancel a request?
Go to your My Requests dashboard. Find the pending request and click Cancel Request. This frees the donor to offer the item to other neighbors without delay.
How do I mark an item as given away?
As the item donor, open your item page and click "Mark Given Away". This updates your community impact statistics, archives the listing from public search, and marks the transaction as complete.
Can I report an inappropriate listing?
Yes! Every item listing page features a "Report Item" button. Select the reason (prohibited item, commercial spam, misleading photos, or offensive language) and submit. Our moderation staff reviews flagged listings promptly.
Community Trust

Safety Guidelines

Keeping our community respectful, reliable, and secure for everyone.

🔒

Don't share your password

Never share your ReuseHub password or personal credentials with anyone. ReuseHub admins will never ask for your password.

🚫

Don't send money for free items

Every item on ReuseHub is 100% free. Never wire money, pay deposits, or pay shipping fees to anyone claiming to be a donor.

👀

Verify the item before collection

Inspect the item in person to ensure it matches the description, particularly if taking electrical or mechanical equipment.

🛡️

Be careful with personal information

Do not post sensitive home details, banking data, or phone numbers publicly on open listings.

🚩

Report suspicious listings or users

If you encounter commercial sales, scams, or rude behavior, use the built-in Report button to notify administrators immediately.

☀️

Arrange collections responsibly

Whenever possible, schedule collections during daylight hours in well-lit, accessible areas or porch pickup spots.
